Planning and Database
**Entity Relationship Diagram ERD**
The components represented in the Race Day ERD include:
1.	USER – contains data about individuals using the system, such as event participants and organisers.
2.	VENUE – holds information about the locations where racing events are conducted.
3.	RACE_EVENT – provides details about organised events for running, walking, or cycling.
4.	RACE – keeps track of specific races or categories within an event.
5.	PARTICIPANT – registers individuals who take part in races.
6.	REGISTRATION – documents the entries of participants into races.
7.	RESULT – records the outcomes or performances of participants in races.
8.	TICKET – maintains information about tickets bought for racing events
